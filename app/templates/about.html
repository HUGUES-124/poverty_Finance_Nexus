Exclusion Priority Index (0–100)

50% poverty rate + 50% lack of reach (total of digital, bank and saving-group users), each min-max scaled across districts. Higher = greater need. Counts are not adjusted for population, so large districts look better reached.

Simulator & risk calculator

Both use simple, transparent rules of thumb for planning discussions. They are not predictions or official targeting tools.
